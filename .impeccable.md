# Impeccable

## Design Context

### Users

Two audiences, watching the same screen for different reasons.

**Celo hackathon judges**, deciding Judges' Favorite by panel. Three people with
distinct lenses: a DevRel lead who cares whether an ordinary person could use
this, a growth lead who cares about real builders and real users in emerging
markets, and a token economist who cares whether the mechanism is
incentive-compatible. They will look at this for perhaps ninety seconds, likely
in a demo video before they ever open the repo.

**Agent developers** evaluating whether to put their own paid endpoint behind
this. They want to know what is enforced on-chain and what is merely bonded,
and they will not take a marketing claim for it.

The job to be done: prove, from live data rather than assertion, that a call
which breached its SLA was never paid for — no refund, no dispute, and no
transaction from the buyer.

### Brand Personality

Cinematic, atmospheric, quietly confident. It should feel like instrumentation
you would trust with money: still in the foreground, alive underneath. The
argument is strong enough that the design does not need to raise its voice.

Never salesy. The strongest line in the project is a flat statement of
consequence — *serving slowly is serving for free* — and the interface should
carry that tone throughout.

### Aesthetic Direction

Dark. This is an operational dashboard read during a demo, and the darkness is
what lets a single accent carry meaning.

Deep near-black tinted toward Celo's yellow hue, with Celo yellow
(`#FCFF52`, roughly `oklch(0.955 0.205 110)`) as a rare accent. The judges are
Celo core contributors, so the brand hue is an advantage — but used at perhaps
five percent of visual weight, reserved for the number that matters and for a
breach's counterpart.

Reference: the atmosphere of walrus.xyz — cinematic depth, ambient drifting
light behind a still foreground, oversized confident type, generous space, one
orchestrated reveal rather than motion everywhere.

Explicit anti-references: aurora purple-to-blue gradients, neon glow on dark,
gradient-filled text, glassmorphism, and identical metric card grids. Those are
the current signature of generated work, and a copy of another project's site
would undercut the innovation the panel is judging.

### Design Principles

1. **Evidence over assertion.** Every claim on the page is next to the live
   number that supports it. The headline metric is the buyer's transaction
   count, because it is falsifiable.
2. **One accent, spent carefully.** Yellow marks the thing being proven.
   Everything else is neutral. An accent everywhere means nothing anywhere.
3. **Still foreground, moving underneath.** Ambient motion belongs to the
   background. Data must never drift, slide, or bounce while being read.
4. **Typography carries the argument.** Hierarchy comes from scale and weight,
   never from decorative fill. Ledger figures are tabular so columns can be
   compared down a row.
5. **Honest about limits.** What is enforced on-chain and what is bonded are
   shown as different things, because conflating them is the failure the whole
   project exists to avoid.

### Type

Display: **Archivo** at expanded widths — wide and stately rather than tall and
loud, which is what "quietly confident" asks for. Body and UI: **Schibsted
Grotesk**. Numerals use Archivo's tabular figures rather than a monospace face,
since monospace as shorthand for "technical" is a cliché and tabular alignment
is the only property actually needed.
